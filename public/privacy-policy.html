1. Introduction

Habbit ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

Google Authentication:

When you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive:

  • Your email address (required)
  • Your full name (optional, if provided in Google account)
  • Your profile picture URL (optional, if provided in Google account)

User Profile Information:

  • Full name (optional)
  • Gender (optional: male, female, or prefer not to say)
  • Any habit data you create (tasks, streaks, preferences)

Communication:

  • Support emails and feedback you send us
  • App crash reports and error logs (if enabled)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Usage Data:

  • App interaction logs (features used, buttons clicked)
  • Session duration and frequency
  • Device information (OS version, device type)
  • App version and build number

Analytical Data:

  • Habit completion rates and trends (stored locally on your device)
  • User engagement metrics (anonymized)

2.3 Information NOT Collected

  • Location data
  • Health data (unless you voluntarily input it in habit descriptions)
  • Biometric data
  • Payment information (app is free)
  • Contacts or calendar data

4. Data Storage & Security

4.1 Where Data Is Stored

  • Authentication: Secure backend servers (encrypted)
  • Personal Profile: Encrypted database storage
  • Habit Data: Encrypted on our servers + local device storage
  • Backups: Regular encrypted backups maintained

4.2 Security Measures

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive data encrypted in database
  • Authentication: JWT tokens with 7-day expiration
  • Access Control: Role-based access to user data
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and penetration testing

4.3 Data Retention

  • Account Active: Data retained while account is active
  • After Deletion: Data deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Backups: May retain backups for up to 90 days (then destroyed)
  • Legal Compliance: Retained as required by law
